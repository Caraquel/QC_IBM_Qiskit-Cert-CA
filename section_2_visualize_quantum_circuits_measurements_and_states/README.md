# Section 2: Visualize Quantum Circuits, Measurements, and States

This section starts the visualization part of the certification preparation path.

## Notebooks

- `2_1_Visualize_Quantum_Circuits.ipynb`
  - Circuit drawing in notebook-friendly forms
  - Text and display-based circuit inspection
- `2_2_Visualize_Measurements.ipynb`
  - Measurement counts and probability histograms
  - Basis-state outcomes from simple circuits
- `2_3_Visualize_Quantum_States.ipynb`
  - Bloch-sphere and qsphere views
  - Relative phase interpretation
- `2_4_Section_2_Exam_Drills.ipynb`
  - Short prediction drills for visualization tasks

## Notes

- The notebooks use `Statevector` and `plot_histogram`, `plot_bloch_multivector`, and `plot_state_qsphere` where appropriate.
- Visualization cells are optional and safely skip if plotting dependencies are unavailable.
